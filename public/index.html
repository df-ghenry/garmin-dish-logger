🤖 Why Android Showed "Impossible to Install" on Local IP & How This Fixes It

When Chrome on Android installs a WebAPK, Google Play Services' cloud server must fetch manifest.json over a publicly reachable https:// domain (it rejects private 192.168.x.x IPs even with Chrome flags).
